usage: pff logs [-h] {list,stats,metrics,cleanup} ...

Comandos para visualizar execution logs e training metrics do PostgreSQL

positional arguments:
  {list,stats,metrics,cleanup}
    list                Listar logs de execucao
    stats               Estatisticas de execucao
    metrics             Visualizar metricas de treinamento
    cleanup             Deletar logs antigos

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
